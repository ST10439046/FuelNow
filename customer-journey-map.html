Customer Steps
  • 1Downloads FuelNow from the App Store or Google Play
  • 2Signs up with name, email & SA mobile number
  • 3Verifies identity via 6-digit OTP (SMS)
  • 4Logs into account and lands on Home screen
Customer Steps
  • 1Checks today's live pump price (e.g. 95 unleaded: R24.67/L)
  • 2Selects fuel type (Petrol 95, 93, Diesel 50ppm, 500ppm)
  • 3Enters Rand amount (e.g. R500) or litre volume (e.g. 30 L)
  • 4Pins delivery location via GPS or picks a saved address
  • 5Taps "Order Now" or schedules a future delivery slot
Customer Steps
  • 1Selects a saved card or adds a new Visa / Mastercard
  • 2Reviews the cost breakdown: fuel + R25 delivery fee + VAT
  • 3Taps "Confirm Order" — payment processed via Payfast
  • 4Receives order confirmation screen (Order #ORD-8822)
Customer Steps
  • 1Waits on "Finding Driver" screen — ETA appears within 90 sec
  • 2Receives push notification: "France Sizwe is on the way"
  • 3Tracks driver live on map with real-time ETA (e.g. 18 min)
  • 4Driver arrives — customer shares 4-digit delivery PIN to verify
  • 5Fuel is dispensed; customer sees "Delivered!" confirmation
Customer Steps
  • 1Rates the driver and experience (1–5 stars)
  • 2Leaves an optional comment for moderation
  • 3Views the full digital receipt (PDF-ready, emailed)
  • 4Checks loyalty progress — earns 50 pts toward free delivery
