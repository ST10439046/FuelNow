Admin Steps
  • 1Logs into the FuelNow Admin web dashboard at admin.fuelnow.co.za
  • 2Reviews the live KPI strip — today's orders, revenue, and active drivers
  • 3Checks the demand heatmap for high-density zones in Durban Metro
  • 4Scans for any overnight SOS alerts or unresolved flags from the previous shift
Admin Steps
  • 1Opens the Orders table and filters for active and pending deliveries
  • 2Identifies any orders flagged as unassigned or running late
  • 3Reviews the Drivers screen to see which drivers are available or offline
  • 4Manually reassigns zones or contacts drivers (e.g. France Sizwe, Durban North) if needed
Admin Steps
  • 1Opens the Fuel Rates screen and verifies the API price sync ran correctly (e.g. Petrol 95 at R24.67/L)
  • 2Overrides a rate manually if the automated feed has failed or is outdated
  • 3Reviews driver compliance tracker for expiry flags on PrDP licences and Hazmat certificates
  • 4Suspends or flags a driver from taking orders until documents are renewed
Admin Steps
  • 1Opens the Reviews panel and filters for flagged or low-rated deliveries (1–2 stars)
  • 2Approves, removes, or escalates problematic reviews for follow-up
  • 3Checks the SOS Alerts monitor for any active driver emergencies
  • 4Dispatches support and marks resolved alerts as closed
Admin Steps
  • 1Opens the Reports screen and selects today's date range
  • 2Pulls the Complete Financial Ledger dataset (orders, revenue, VAT)
  • 3Exports Driver Performance report to Excel for the owner's afternoon review
  • 4Logs out and hands over to the evening shift administrator
