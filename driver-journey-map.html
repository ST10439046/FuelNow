Driver Steps
  • 1Opens FuelNow Driver app and logs in with ID and PIN
  • 2Sets status to "Available" to begin receiving orders
  • 3Reviews available orders on map and list view
  • 4Checks today's fuel load and tank capacity on truck FN-TRK-004
Driver Steps
  • 1Opens order card — views fuel type, volume (e.g. 40 L Petrol 95), distance (6.2 km) and customer name
  • 2Calculates whether the job is worthwhile given current route
  • 3Taps Accept — or skips to view the next available job
Driver Steps
  • 1Follows turn-by-turn navigation to customer address in Morningside, Durban
  • 2Updates job status to "En Route" via the app
  • 3Updates status to "Arrived" on reaching the location
  • 4Updates status to "Dispensing" once pumping begins
Driver Steps
  • 1Captures a proof-of-delivery photo via the app camera
  • 2Prompts customer to share their 4-digit verification PIN
  • 3Enters customer PIN to confirm and close the order
  • 4Receives "Order Complete" confirmation — job logs to earnings
Driver Steps
  • 1Views today's earnings summary and completed deliveries (e.g. R1 240 across 5 jobs)
  • 2Moves to next available order, OR marks status as "Offline" to end shift
  • 3If a truck fault or safety concern arises: taps SOS button to alert operations
